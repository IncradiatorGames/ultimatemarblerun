Delete your account

This page is for Ultimate Marble Run by Incradiator Games.

In the game

Open the game, open the Menu, tap your profile picture, tap the three dots on your page, then Delete account.

Without the game

Email IncradiatorGames@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and player name
  • Machines you published, and their pictures
  • Your likes and reports

What we keep

Nothing on our servers - your account, name, machines (with every saved version and picture), likes, reviews, reports and bug reports are all removed together. Purchases are made through Google Play, so Google keeps its own record of them; deleting your account doesn't refund or remove a purchase.
